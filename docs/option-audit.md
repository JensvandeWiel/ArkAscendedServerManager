## Notes

- `status=covered-by-cli` means the wiki lists the key under `[ServerSettings]` but the app sets it on the launch line (ports, passwords). That is intentional, but it must never be written in both places.
- `status=missing-unknown` options are candidates for an experimental group; do not ship them as normal settings.
- Table options are tracked in `docs/server-configuration-parity-plan.md` §7.
- Stale `implemented` flags are expected until phase 0 finishes; the scraper preserves them.
